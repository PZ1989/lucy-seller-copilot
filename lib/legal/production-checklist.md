# Legal production checklist

Internal implementation notes; do not expose in end-user UI.

- [ ] Define operational retention periods for account data, chat history, OAuth data, and security logs.
- [ ] Verify all live subprocessors and vendor data-processing terms before production.
- [ ] Verify international transfer safeguards for EEA data.
- [ ] Confirm the live payment processor and update payment wording accordingly.
- [ ] Finalize the EU withdrawal/consent flow before immediate digital-service activation.
- [ ] Implement GDPR access, correction, export, restriction, and deletion request handling.
- [ ] Implement account deletion, chat-history clearing, and disconnect operations.
- [ ] Verify cookie and storage technologies; add a consent banner only if required.
- [ ] Verify the security and personal-data breach response workflow.
- [ ] Confirm whether DPO or EU representative obligations apply.
- [ ] Confirm consumer complaint and dispute information required for target markets.
- [ ] Confirm the exact EU/EEA withdrawal acknowledgement and consent flow before immediate digital-service activation.
- [ ] Ensure Terms acceptance remains separate from optional marketing consent.
- [ ] Verify material policy-change notices and any consent re-confirmation requirements.
